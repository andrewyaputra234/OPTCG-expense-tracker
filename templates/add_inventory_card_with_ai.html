Tips for Best Results
Image Upload Tips:
  • Use clear, well-lit photos
  • Ensure card text is readable
  • Include the full card in frame
  • Avoid glare or shadows
  • Multiple cards in one image are supported
Description Tips:
  • Include card number for accurate identification
  • Specify purchase price in Japanese Yen (¥)
  • Mention quantity if more than 1
  • Include any special notes or conditions
  • Use format: "OP01-025 Monkey D. Luffy SR ¥800"
